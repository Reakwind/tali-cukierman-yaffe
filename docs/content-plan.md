## 3. Study findings and exact gaps

Sources were checked on 2026-10-31. Registry update dates below are the record's

### STAY-STRONG — identified

- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06497595][stay], protocol identifier `1072-24-SMC`.
  Last update posted **2026-07-21**; overall status **RECRUITING**.
- Randomized research asking whether exercise and nutritional support alongside
  semaglutide affect muscle mass, strength and physical function in older adults
  with type 2 diabetes and overweight/obesity. This is a research question, not an
  established benefit.
- Broad registry population: age 65+, type 2 diabetes, BMI ≥27. The record has
  substantial additional exclusions; these headline criteria cannot determine
  whether someone can participate.
- Registry treatment period: 26 weeks. It describes screening and assessments at
  baseline, weeks 8 and 16, and week 26, with additional contact/medication
  arrangements. The intervention includes a supervised online exercise session
  plus two independent sessions weekly, nutritional support and food diaries.
- Assessment burden includes physical/cognitive tests, imaging and laboratory
  sampling. Muscle biopsies are optional according to the record; declining them
  does not remove a participant. Do not omit these details from a reviewed
  participant explanation or describe every procedure as mandatory.
- Lead sponsor: Sheba; collaborator: Hadassah. Site listings differ: **Sheba
  recruiting**, **Hadassah not yet recruiting**. Neither listing substitutes for
  direct operational confirmation or recruitment-advertising approval.

**Supply/confirm:** current site availability and date; preferred public title in
both languages; approved participant leaflet/recruitment wording; actual visit
lengths, location and any extra visits; exercise/dietitian commitment; required
versus optional tests; reviewed eligibility, risks, costs/reimbursement and what
the study provides; coordinator route; approved collaborators/funder credits.
Public-facing documents or factual bullets are enough—do not send confidential
protocol correspondence or participant records.

### BIRAX — project confirmed, formal match still provisional

  project developing a machine-learning platform to assess physical function in

**Supply/confirm:** whether the Steve Bain/Swansea award is the intended project;

site status; broad population and participation commitment; approved public
summary and recruitment information if applicable; related outputs/funder credits.
Use a descriptive study title with a BIRAX funding credit rather than relying on

### GRACE-780G — name confirmed by the owner

Owner confirmation 2026-10-31: **GRACE-780G** is the official study name to
showcase on the site alongside STAY-STRONG and BIRAX. It is the completed MiniMed
780G trial below; the device name is descriptive, not the study's public identity.

- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06236256][minimed], protocol identifier `0205-23-SMC`.
  Last update posted **2026-07-21**; status **COMPLETED**, completion date
  **2025-08-20**, actual enrollment **42**.
- Sheba randomized study comparing MiniMed 780G advanced hybrid closed-loop
  therapy with usual injection/pump treatment in older adults with type 1 diabetes,
  with approximately 12-month follow-up. The abstract describes ages 60+; the
  registry uses both a minimum age of 60 and the wording "over 60".
- [EASD abstract LBA 19][grace] calls it **GRACE-780G** and explicitly cites
  **NCT06236256**, resolving the connection between those public names. The owner
  should still confirm this is the intended "MiniMed 780 study".
- Authors include H. Shimon, Y. Alt, E. Cohen and T. Cukierman-Yaffe. The abstract
  reports glycaemic findings and Medtronic unrestricted-grant support. Keep
  funding support distinct from the registry's lead sponsor, Sheba.
- The registry has no posted structured results, but a public results abstract
  exists. Do not say "no results exist", describe a conference abstract as a full
  peer-reviewed trial paper, or infer cognitive/physical improvement from the
  reported glycaemic findings. No full GRACE-780G results paper was verified in
  this search; that does not establish that none exists.

**Supply/confirm:** relevant published article/preprint/poster links if available;
team-approved summary of findings and limitations; study-team contributions and
funding wording; whether any distinct follow-on study exists. A follow-on needs its
own identity/status, not a recruiting badge on this completed trial. Do not supply
unpublished results or confidential manuscripts for the public repository.
