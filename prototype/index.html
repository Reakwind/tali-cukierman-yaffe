We study healthy aging with diabetes.

Our research aims to better understand cognitive function, frailty and the use of diabetes technologies among older adults, with a strong focus on early detection and prevention.

Research

Cognitive function

We study how diabetes affects thinking, memory and processing speed in later life, and how to detect changes early. Our group developed a digital version of the Digit Symbol Substitution Test, a short test of processing speed, for older adults with diabetes.

Frailty

We study frailty through physical capacity: strength, fitness and balance. We measure everyday movement with wearable sensors, develop machine-learning methods to identify people at risk, and test whether exercise and nutrition can help preserve muscle.

Diabetes technologies

We study how older adults use diabetes technologies, such as automated insulin delivery, and how they relate to cognitive and physical function.

6 Vital

A digital health platform created by Prof. Cukierman-Yaffe, with digital cognitive measurement, a patient app and a care-giver tool. 6-vital.com
