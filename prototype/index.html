We study healthy aging with diabetes.

Our research aims to better understand cognitive function, frailty and the use of diabetes technologies among older adults, with a strong focus on early detection and prevention.

Research

We see diabetes as a condition that speeds up aging. Cognitive decline and loss of physical function are complications of diabetes, and like other complications they can be detected early and prevented. Care should follow a person's physical and cognitive capacity rather than their age, which calls for measures that work in routine care.

Our research is part of the Center for Successful Aging with Diabetes at Sheba Medical Center, which brings together clinical care in the Diabetes 360 clinic for people aged 60 and over, training, and research.

Ongoing study Publication

  1. Cognitive function

    Thinking and memory shape how people manage diabetes day to day. We develop measures of cognitive function that are practical in the clinic and sensitive to change over time, including a digital version of the Digit Symbol Substitution Test, a short test of processing speed.

    • Publication: Digital symbol test2024
    • Publication: REWIND2020

  2. Frailty

    We look at frailty through physical capacity: strength, fitness and balance. We measure everyday movement with wearable sensors, develop machine-learning methods to identify people at risk earlier, and test whether exercise and nutrition can help preserve muscle.

    • BIRAXOngoing study
    • STAY-STRONGOngoing study
    • Publication: Wearable sensors2023
    • Publication: Sarcopenia review2026

  3. Diabetes technologies

    We study how older adults use diabetes technologies, such as automated insulin delivery, and how they relate to cognitive and physical function.

    • Publication: GRACE-780G2026
    • Publication: Technology in older-adult care2023

How we measure

We developed 6 Vital, our own platform for running studies and measuring people outside the clinic. A phone app records movement through the phone's sensors and runs our digital Digit Symbol Substitution Test, so participants can be followed remotely, quickly and with little staff time. With collaborators, we develop machine-learning models to analyse the app's data. Our aim is assessment that is fast, inexpensive and accurate enough to support early detection and intervention, without adding to the load on stretched health services. 6-vital.com
